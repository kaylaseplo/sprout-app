# Sprout — Product Brief

**The teacher's assistant that never clocks out.**

---

## The Problem

Early childhood education is one of the most demanding and underserved professions. Nursery teachers are responsible for the development, safety, and documentation of 8-15 children simultaneously — yet the tools they're given are built for administrators and parents, not for them.

The result:
- Activity planning happens from scratch every day, relying on personal memory and Pinterest
- Documentation (photos, updates, developmental notes) is a manual burden that competes with being present with children
- Institutional knowledge — what works with which age group, which activities land, how to handle specific behaviors — lives in individual teachers' heads and walks out the door when they leave
- New and junior teachers have no in-the-moment support when they face challenging behavior or developmental questions

Existing tools like Brightwheel and HiMama solve for parent communication and billing. Nobody is solving for the teacher's daily workflow.

---

## The Solution

Sprout is an AI-powered classroom assistant for nursery and daycare teachers. It reduces the documentation burden, supports teachers in the moment, and builds institutional knowledge over time — so great teaching doesn't disappear when staff turn over.

**Core promise: The teacher's assistant that never clocks out.**

---

## Who It's For

**Primary buyer:** Nursery/daycare director
- Responsible for staff quality, parent satisfaction, and retention
- Feels the pain of staff turnover and lost institutional knowledge
- Wants to give teachers tools that make their jobs easier and more professional

**Primary user:** Classroom teacher
- Overwhelmed by documentation requirements
- Needs in-the-moment support, not end-of-day paperwork
- Wants to be present with children, not buried in their phone

## Core Features

### 1. Smart Photo Documentation
The biggest friction in real-time parent updates is the manual work after the photo is taken — tagging children, writing captions, uploading.

**The Sprout workflow:**
- Teacher opens camera in app and says a child's name (or names) while shooting
- App auto-tags the photo to the correct child profiles
- AI drafts a caption based on what's visible in the photo and the current activity context
- Teacher reviews in 2 seconds and sends
- Parent receives the update in real time

**Fallback:** If no voice trigger, app shows class roster as large tap-friendly buttons for fast manual tagging.

**Result:** Photo documentation goes from 2-3 minutes of manual work to under 5 seconds.

---

### 2. Activity Generator
Teachers plan activities daily for their specific age group with whatever supplies their classroom has. Sprout generates age-appropriate, supply-aware activity ideas instantly.

**Inputs:**
- Classroom age group (set once in profile)
- Energy level of the group today
- Time available
- Indoor or outdoor
- Supplies available in the room
- Activity history (what they've done recently — avoids repeats)

**Output:** 3 tailored activity ideas with instructions and developmental rationale.

**Over time:** Sprout learns what works in each classroom. Teachers can rate activities, and the system builds a knowledge base of what resonates with which age group.

---

### 3. Behavior & Development Chat
When a child is hitting, refusing to nap, or struggling socially — teachers need guidance in the moment, not a textbook. Sprout provides an always-available chat assistant trained on early childhood development and classroom behavior management.

**Example queries:**
- "A 2 year old keeps biting when frustrated, what should I do?"
- "How do I handle a child who won't separate from their parent at drop-off?"
- "What's age-appropriate sharing behavior for 18 month olds?"

The assistant gives practical, actionable guidance grounded in early childhood best practices — not generic parenting advice.

---

### 4. Classroom Knowledge Base
Every activity rating, every behavior note, every documented moment builds a classroom profile over time. When a new teacher joins, they inherit the knowledge of everyone who came before them — not a blank slate.

**What it captures:**
- Activities that worked well (and which age groups loved them)
- Supplies the classroom has access to
- Behavioral patterns and what strategies helped
- Developmental milestones observed

---

## What Sprout Is Not

- Not a parent communication platform (Brightwheel owns this — integrate, don't compete)
- Not a billing or admin tool
- Not a facial recognition system — child privacy is non-negotiable
- Not another app teachers have to remember to use — it has to fit into existing workflow

---

## The Business Model

**B2B SaaS, sold to nursery directors.**

- Per-classroom or per-staff pricing
- Director dashboard showing usage across classrooms
- Potential to sell to nursery chains / franchises at scale (one sale = 20+ classrooms)

**Why directors buy:**
- Reduces staff turnover impact (knowledge stays in the system)
- Improves parent satisfaction (more frequent, richer updates)
- Makes teachers' jobs easier and more professional (retention benefit)
- Demonstrates investment in staff development

## Why Now

- AI tools have matured to the point where voice tagging, image captioning, and behavior guidance are all feasible at low cost
- Early childhood education is facing a staffing crisis — anything that reduces burnout and makes the job easier is a compelling sell
- No incumbent is focused on the teacher experience layer
